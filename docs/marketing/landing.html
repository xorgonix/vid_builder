Develop & Outline

Turn an idea into an accepted brief, then a section → scene → shot plan. Every shot has a purpose and a visible change.

Board, Blocking & Produce

Stage every shot cheaply, then freeze a specification into a generation request — candidates, quality, and where it runs.

Review, Edit & Compute

Compare candidates with receipts, approve the best, assemble the cut, and plan where the costly work really runs.

What you get

Storyboard-first

Start · Change · End cards. The end must visibly pay off the start; the studio tells you when it doesn't.

Blocking editor

Move subjects, gaze, props and camera; get a change analysis. Saved as structured data, not throwaway images.

Prompt layers

Style, character, location, motion — inherit, append, replace, suppress or lock. See the exact compiled prompt first.

Bring your own workflows

Author and test graphs in ComfyUI; import them as immutable versions. vid-build binds only the inputs you confirm.

Evidence-based review

Full-size comparison with a receipt per candidate: model, seed, workflow, time. Select, approve, or approve with a recorded exception.

Compute you control

Estimates from your measured runs. Bundle work into a campaign, preflight it, then reconcile what you actually spent.

Honest status

A working vertical slice — not a mockup

The full path works today: create a project, develop a brief, outline, block, generate on a live ComfyUI, review and approve, edit, and export an MP4. It has been exercised end-to-end against a real local stack (LM Studio planner, ComfyUI on an RTX 3090, FFmpeg).

Still maturing: desktop packaging, rented/API execution adapters, audio. We would rather tell you what works than what sounds impressive.

Questions

Do I need ComfyUI?

No. You can develop, outline, storyboard and block without it. ComfyUI is needed only for generated frames.

Do I need to be online?

No. vid-build is local-first. It talks to your machine's services over loopback.

Will it replace my ComfyUI workflows?

No — the opposite. You author and test graphs in ComfyUI; vid-build imports them as immutable versions and binds only the inputs you confirm.

Can it make the film automatically?

Deliberately not. It is built so a human decides, selects and approves. That is the feature.

Is my client material safe?

It stays on your machine. Local ComfyUI is reached over loopback and never exposed; LAN review pairs devices to vid-build, not to your model server.

templ generate
go run ./cmd/vidbuild -open
