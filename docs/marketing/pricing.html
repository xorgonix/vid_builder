Pricing questions

Is this really free?

The Local tier is free during early access. We do not meter your local renders — that would be absurd for software running on your own GPU.

Why are Studio prices "to be announced"?

We are validating pricing with early studios. We would rather publish a real price than a placeholder we would have to change.

Do you take a cut of rented compute?

When the Coordinator tier introduces rented execution, it will be billed with transparent reconciliation — you see the actual provider cost and our fee separately. No hidden markup.

Can I use my own API keys?

Yes. Credentials are referenced, never stored as raw secrets in the database, HTML or receipts.

What if I cancel?

Your projects are yours: a SQLite database and a media folder. They keep working, and you can export project packages at any time.
