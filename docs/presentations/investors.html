vid-build 0.01

The compute-native video studio

Local-first. Human-authorised. Built to own your workflows, your lineage and your compute economics.

AI video has a workflow problem

  • Model quality exploded; production discipline did not.
  • Creators juggle prompt tabs, model zoo, and spreadsheets to guess GPU cost.
  • Decisions and recipes vanish — you can’t reproduce last week’s good take.
  • Cloud tools are convenient but pull creative work, client material and cost control out of your hands.

Our insight

Making video is production, not prompting.

  • Decisions have to be visible, attributed and reversible.
  • You should see the film before it exists as pixels.
  • Compute is a budget with options — local, rented, or API.

What vid-build is

For creators

  • Idea → brief → outline → storyboard → shots → review → edit
  • Every shot has a purpose and a visible change

For studios & clients

  • Workspaces, roles and approval trails
  • Runs on your hardware, over your models

A desktop studio that is also a real local web app.

Why now

  • Open models for image and video are good enough to build on — and keep improving.
  • Owning a GPU (or renting one) is now an economically real alternative to per-render SaaS pricing.
  • Studios and agencies face client-privacy and provenance requirements that cloud-only flows struggle to meet.
  • ComfyUI proved power users will author their own pipelines — but it is not a studio.

The awkward middle of AI video — deciding, comparing and paying — is exactly what we productise.

The compute story

  • Same frozen request can run on a local GPU, a rented worker, or an API.
  • Estimates come from your measured runs, not vendor benchmarks.
  • Campaigns bundle compatible work: preflight → confirm → run → reconcile actual spend.
  • Two GPUs are modelled as independent workers — never “add the VRAM together”.

Compute economics is not a settings page. It is the business model.

Privacy & enterprise readiness

  • Workspace boundaries enforced in the services, not just the UI.
  • Local ComfyUI stays on loopback; phones/tablets pair to vid-build, never to the model server.
  • Optional LAN review with per-device sessions; off by default and revocable.
  • Coordinated backups and portable client project packages.

Business-model hypotheses to validate

  • Open core: local studio free; paid team/server coordinator and collaboration.
  • Compute margin: convenience layer for rented GPUs and API routing, billed with transparent reconciliation.
  • Workflow marketplace: packaged, validated workflows and style kits.
  • Studio support: deployment, backup/restore and compliance assistance.

Moat

  • Workflow-binding standard: graphs stay owned by ComfyUI; our semantic bindings make any workflow portable across models and vendors.
  • Provenance graph: decision → spec → workflow hash → attempt → asset → approval. Switching tools means abandoning your own production history.
  • Cost advantage: measured, reconciled compute beats opaque credits.

Where it is today real, not a mockup

  • Working vertical slice: workspace → project → brief → outline → blocking → generation on a live ComfyUI → review/approval → edit → MP4 export.
  • Compute planner and campaigns; backup/verify/restore; LAN pairing; model profiles; annotations.
  • Unit-tested core (blocking renderer, workflow bindings, ids, config) and a clean build/vet/test.

Built from a full written specification; every capability above was exercised against a real local stack.
